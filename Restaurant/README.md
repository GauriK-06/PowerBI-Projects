🍽️ Restaurant Sales & Operational Performance Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Restaurant Sales & Operational Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze restaurant sales, customer behavior, order trends, and operational performance. It provides meaningful insights into revenue generation, popular menu categories, ordering channels, and delivery efficiency to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and order volume.
- Track quarterly revenue trends.
- Understand hourly order demand.
- Compare performance across Delivery App, Dine-In, and Takeaway channels.
- Identify high-performing menu categories.
- Monitor average delivery time, customer ratings, and repeat customer percentage.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

- Total Revenue: 2.60M
