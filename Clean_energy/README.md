# Clean Energy & Grid Performance Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze clean energy generation, grid performance, and energy source trends. 
It provides insights into total energy generation, CO₂ emissions avoided, capacity factor, curtailment rate, and average energy tariffs.

## Key Features

* **KPI Cards:** Total Generation, CO₂ Avoided, Average Capacity Factor, Average Curtailment Rate, and Average Tariff.
* **Generation Trends:** Compares yearly energy generation across different energy sources and plant capacity factors.
* **Energy Mix Analysis:** Visualizes the contribution of different energy sources to total generation.
* **State-wise Performance:** Highlights the top power-generating states.
* **Energy Source Trends:** Tracks energy generation patterns from 2023 to 2026.
* **Interactive Filters:** Filter data by date, grid zone, and renewable energy status.
