# 🏥 Public Health Access & Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze public healthcare accessibility, facility performance, patient footfall, waiting times, and healthcare costs. It provides insights into healthcare infrastructure, patient satisfaction, bed availability, and insurance coverage to support data-driven analysis of public health services.

## 📊 Key Features

* **KPI Overview:** Displays total healthcare facilities, total bed capacity, average waiting time, staff vacancy rate, and average out-of-pocket cost.
* **Healthcare Facility Analysis:** Compares facility types across rural and urban localities.
* **Yearly Patient Footfall:** Tracks changes in patient visits over time.
* **Ailment Category Analysis:** Identifies categories contributing to monthly patient footfall.
* **Wait Time & Satisfaction Analysis:** Examines the relationship between patient waiting times, satisfaction scores, and locality.
* **Out-of-Pocket Cost Analysis:** Compares average healthcare costs across insurance schemes.
* **Bed Availability:** Visualizes the distribution of available beds across rural and urban areas.
* **Interactive Filters:** Allows filtering by year, state, and locality.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Data Cleaning and Transformation
* Data Visualization

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze healthcare infrastructure and service accessibility.
* Compare healthcare facilities across rural and urban areas.
* Monitor patient footfall and waiting time trends.
* Understand healthcare costs across insurance schemes.
* Explore bed availability and patient satisfaction indicators.
